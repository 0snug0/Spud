# SPD-005 — Obsidian readability

## Brief
Eric opened the vault and found the ledger hard to read: no entry point, a board made of raw lines, dense working notes, and templates showing up as notes. Fix the cheap things without pre-empting the DB spike. Done when: a Home note explains how to read names and notes, the board is tables, Bases views list every spudagent and every ticket, and the templates are out of the way.

## Size, persona and model decision
No spudagent: every deliverable is a Spud-owned file (`ledger/`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/`). Done directly by Eric's session at his request, at 2026-09-12T12:57.

## Outcome
Added `ledger/Home.md` (entry point and legend), rewrote `ledger/BOARD.md` as three tables, added `ledger/Fleet.base` (Active and All views over spudagent frontmatter) and `ledger/Tickets.base` (Open, All, and Reports views), moved the templates to `ledger/_templates/` and excluded that folder in the local Obsidian settings. Tickets gained a `lead` property, backfilled on SPD-001 and SPD-004. CLAUDE.md, the agent definition, and the spec point at the new paths. Not verified in-app by this session: Obsidian renders the `.base` files; Eric checks that on opening. Follow-up from Eric's first look: `origin: proposal:[[...]]` rendered as a URL scheme in Obsidian, so origin is now `eric` or `proposal` and the link moved to a `proposed_by` property; contractors use `persona: contractor` plus `agent_type` for the same reason. Second follow-up: `BOARD.md` and `Tickets.base` replaced by `ledger/Board.base`, one table over ticket frontmatter with status as a column; Spud now moves tickets by editing their `status`. Third follow-up: team folders moved from `ledger/tickets/SPD-nnn/` to `ledger/teams/SPD-nnn/` so a ticket note and its team folder no longer share a name in the file explorer; links are unchanged because Obsidian resolves the `SPD-nnn/Name` suffix.
