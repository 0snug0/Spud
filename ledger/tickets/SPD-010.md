# SPD-010 — Per-ticket Team card and Fleet.base#Team view

## Brief
Eric's stated need, 2026-09-12: click a ticket and see the lead, every spudagent under it with persona, model and status, and a summary of what they worked on and built. The spike's question 8 answer ([[SPD-006]]) proposes two pieces. (1) A `Team` view in `Fleet.base` filtered `ticket == this`, embedded in every ticket note as `![[Fleet.base#Team]]`; this needs no database and can land first on today's frontmatter. (2) A generated `## Team` table written by `spud render` from `members`: lead first, lineage order, persona (with `agent_type` for contractors), model tier and resolved model, status, run and duration, tokens and tool uses, and a "worked on / built" paragraph from `members.summary` with fallbacks to the first paragraph of `result` and then to `return_text`.

Part 2 depends on [[SPD-007]].

Done when: opening any ticket note in Obsidian shows the team table and the embedded view, and the summary column reads as a sentence about the work, not a status line.

## Size, persona and model decision
Two sequential children of Spud: a designer (opus) specifies the table layout and the summary rules so the card reads well in Obsidian, then an engineer (opus) implements the `Fleet.base` view and the renderer part.
