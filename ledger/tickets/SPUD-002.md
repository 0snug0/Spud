# SPUD-002 — Add an index page for docs/toy/

## Brief
`docs/toy/` has no entry point: no `README.md` or `index.md`. Once more than one toy doc lands there, a reader arriving at the folder has nothing linking them together. Proposed by [[SPUD-001/Rosara|Rosara]] during [[SPUD-001]] (it was told about the gap as a verification wrinkle and correctly proposed rather than built), escalated unchanged by [[SPUD-001/Kestrel|Kestrel]], created here by Spud at the suggested P3. Done when: `docs/toy/README.md` exists, lists every file in `docs/toy/` with one line each, and nothing else in the repo changes.

## Size, persona and model decision
Small: one scout on haiku, no children. A one-file listing is a lookup plus a few lines of prose; nothing here is expensive if wrong.
